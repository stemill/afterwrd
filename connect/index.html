Connecting to Cloudflare…

Connect this afterwrd site to Cloudflare?

This lets that site make and change Workers and databases in your Cloudflare account for an hour, so only ever for your own site: check this is its address. Cloudflare's next page says what's being asked for, but not which site it's for: only this page does. If this isn't your own site, close this tab.

Let your afterwrd site keep itself up to date?

It's asking Cloudflare for lasting Background Access to change your Workers (Workers Editor), so it can install each new afterwrd release by itself once it's been out for a few days. Unlike installing, this doesn't end after an hour: it lasts until you turn it off. Nothing else: it can't make or read databases, read your account's settings or make tokens. Turn it off any time on the site's Update page, or in Cloudflare (My Profile → Access Management → Connected Applications). Only continue if this is your own site's address.

Let your friends' afterwrd sites check yours by themselves?

It's asking Cloudflare for lasting Background Access to read the code of every Worker in your Cloudflare account (not only afterwrd's), and nothing else: it can't change anything, or read your databases or your account's settings. Your site hands a friend's site that asks a one-hour, read-only pass from it, so they can check your site is exactly the published afterwrd, now and then, without you doing anything. Turn it off any time on the site's Update page, or in Cloudflare (My Profile → Access Management → Connected Applications). Only continue if this is your own site's address.

Let this friend's afterwrd site check yours?

It's asking to read your Cloudflare account for an hour: your site's code, and the account's settings and log of changes. It can't change anything. Only continue if this is the site of a friend who asked to check yours.

This page passes you between your afterwrd site and Cloudflare. It keeps nothing. How it works.
