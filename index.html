afterwrd

A small social network of your own

Post, share photos and talk with friends who have their own afterwrd site, and, if you like, bring your Facebook years along. It runs in your own free Cloudflare account, so no one else holds your data and there's nothing to pay. A few minutes, on a phone or a computer.

Curious or cautious? How it works explains where your site runs, what it talks to, how signing in and friends work, and how to check all of it in the public code. Or, in plain words: how afterwrd is different from Facebook.

  1. Make a free Cloudflare account

    An email address is all it asks for. Don't add a card; afterwrd stays within the free plan. Confirm your email from the message Cloudflare sends, and stay signed in.

    Sign up to Cloudflare

    Already have a Cloudflare account? If you use it for other things, make a new one just for afterwrd: installing asks for permission over every Worker and database in the account, for an hour, so in an account of its own that's only afterwrd. Otherwise, sign in.

  2. Install

    Once you're signed in to Cloudflare in this browser, press Install, and on Cloudflare's page press Allow (it lets afterwrd's installer make your site, for an hour; nothing is kept). Give your name (your address is made from it) and a couple of minutes later your site is ready: make yourself its owner with a passkey (your face, fingerprint or device PIN; no password). Then you're in: write a post, add photos, and invite friends from Friends. It all works on a phone.

    Install

    Careful? Check your install before you start: in your Cloudflare dashboard, or by asking a friend with afterwrd to check it (Update → Get your site checked).

    Prefer not to use afterwrd's installer? Use Cloudflare's own Deploy page

    This puts the installer in your own account first, so nothing of afterwrd's touches your connection to Cloudflare. It's easiest on a computer.

    1. Open Cloudflare's Deploy page with afterwrd's installer ready. Leave the name as it is and press Deploy. (If Cloudflare asks you to create an account, you aren't signed in in this browser.)
    2. Press Visit (on a phone, it's in the ⋯ menu at the top). The installer asks for your site's address, then Connect Cloudflare and Allow, installs afterwrd and removes itself.
    Or by hand, if the Deploy page doesn't work
    1. Go to Workers & Pages (Compute → Workers & Pages in the left-hand menu), press Create application, then Start with Hello World. Change the Worker name to afterwrd, leave Protect with Cloudflare Access off and press Deploy.
    2. Copy the installer, then in your new Worker press Edit code, select everything in the editor (⌘A or Ctrl+A), paste over it and press Deploy. Then open the Worker's address.

  3. Bring your Facebook years (optional, any time)

    In Facebook's Download your information, choose your profile, All available information, format JSON and media quality High. Facebook emails you when it's ready, which can take from minutes to a day. Then choose Import in afterwrd's menu (behind your picture), pick the zip file(s) and tick what to bring. Your posts, photos and comments go under Memories, and your Facebook friends list shows who to invite.

Keeping it safe

  • Turn on two-factor authentication for your Cloudflare account (My Profile → Authentication). Anyone who gets into that account can see your whole site.
  • Open device links only on your own phone or computer. They let a new device in as you, so they work once, for an hour. Your devices can cancel them, remove a passkey (signing that device out) or sign out everywhere.
  • Approve only friends you know. Connect and invite links can be passed on, so each one only asks: nothing is shared until you approve, and you can check the site address first.
  • Photo locations are removed from photos you post, and left out of a Facebook import unless you choose to include them: they can show where you live.
  • Keep it up to date from the Update page (marked when there's a new version), so security fixes reach your site. Home says so when an update fixes a security problem. To hear about every release, follow the releases feed or Watch the repository on GitHub (Custom → Releases).
  • Watch for new devices. When a passkey is added, Home on your other devices says so for two weeks. If it wasn't you, remove it in Your devices.
