Install heybert

Your Facebook archive as a private website that only people you invite can see. It runs in your own free Cloudflare account: no one else holds your data, and there's nothing to pay. About 15 minutes on a computer.

  1. Ask Facebook for your download

    In Facebook's Download your information, choose your profile, All available information, format JSON and media quality High. Facebook emails you when it's ready, which can take from minutes to a day. You'll need the zip file(s) at step 5.

  2. Make a free Cloudflare account

    An email address is all it asks for. Don't add a card; heybert stays within the free plan.

    Sign up to Cloudflare

  3. Create a Worker

    In Cloudflare, go to Workers & Pages and press Create application, then Start with Hello World. Change the Worker name to heybert (it becomes your site's address), leave Protect with Cloudflare Access off and press Deploy.

    Open Workers & Pages

    If the link lands elsewhere: Compute → Workers & Pages in the left-hand menu.

  4. Paste in the installer

    Copy the installer, then in your new Worker press Edit code, select everything in the editor (⌘A or Ctrl+A), paste over it and press Deploy.

    See what you're pasting (heybert __VERSION__)

  5. Open your Worker and follow the installer

    Press Visit (or open https://heybert.<your-name>.workers.dev). The installer walks you through the rest: a Cloudflare token it needs once, a passkey for you (your face, fingerprint or device PIN; no password), then importing your Facebook zip and inviting people.

Keeping it safe

  • Turn on two-factor authentication for your Cloudflare account (My Profile → Authentication). Anyone who gets into that account can see your whole archive.
  • Delete the installer's token when it's finished (it reminds you). heybert doesn't keep it, but while it exists it can change your Workers.
  • Send invite links somewhere only that person will see them. Whoever opens a link first gets in. Under People → Devices & links you can cancel links that haven't been used, delete a passkey or sign someone out.
  • Photo locations are left out unless you choose to include them when importing: they can show where you live.
  • Keep it up to date from the Update page, so security fixes reach your site.

The installer downloads heybert __VERSION__ from its published release and checks every file against the fingerprint __MANIFEST_SHORT__… before installing anything. The token is only used while installing; you can delete it afterwards in Cloudflare under My Profile → API Tokens. Source code.
