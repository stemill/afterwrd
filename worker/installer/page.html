afterwrd

A small social network of your own

Post, share photos and talk with friends who have their own afterwrd site, and, if you like, bring your Facebook years along. It runs in your own free Cloudflare account, so no one else holds your data and there's nothing to pay. A few minutes on a computer.

Curious or cautious? How it works explains where your site runs, what it talks to, how signing in and friends work, and how to check all of it in the public code.

  1. Make a free Cloudflare account

    An email address is all it asks for. Don't add a card; afterwrd stays within the free plan. A new account just for afterwrd is best, so your site can safely update itself in one click. Confirm your email from the message Cloudflare sends, and stay signed in.

    Sign up to Cloudflare

    Already have an account just for afterwrd? Sign in.

  2. Install on Cloudflare

    Once you're signed in, this opens Cloudflare with afterwrd's installer ready to deploy. Leave the name as it is (you'll choose your site's address in a moment) and press Deploy.

    Install on Cloudflare

    If Cloudflare asks you to create an account, you aren't signed in in this browser: sign in, then press the button again.

    If the button doesn't work, do it by hand
    1. Go to Workers & Pages (Compute → Workers & Pages in the left-hand menu), press Create application, then Start with Hello World. Change the Worker name to afterwrd, leave Protect with Cloudflare Access off and press Deploy.
    2. Copy the installer, then in your new Worker press Edit code, select everything in the editor (⌘A or Ctrl+A), paste over it and press Deploy.
    See the installer (afterwrd __VERSION__)

  3. Open your Worker and follow the installer

    Press Visit. The installer walks you through the rest: your site's address (afterwrd unless you choose otherwise), a Cloudflare token (leave Remember it ticked, so updates are one click), then a passkey for you (your face, fingerprint or device PIN; no password). Then you're in: write a post, add photos, and invite friends from Friends.

  4. Bring your Facebook years (optional, any time)

    In Facebook's Download your information, choose your profile, All available information, format JSON and media quality High. Facebook emails you when it's ready, which can take from minutes to a day. Then choose Import in afterwrd's menu (behind your picture), pick the zip file(s) and tick what to bring. Your posts, photos and comments go under Memories, and your Facebook friends list shows who to invite.

Keeping it safe

  • Turn on two-factor authentication for your Cloudflare account (My Profile → Authentication). Anyone who gets into that account can see your whole site.
  • Only let your site remember the token if the Cloudflare account is just for afterwrd. The token can change any Worker in the account. If the account has other things in it, untick Remember it, delete the token after installing (My Profile → API Tokens), and make a new one each time you update.
  • Open device links only on your own phone or computer. They let a new device in as you, so they work once, for an hour. Your devices can cancel them, remove a passkey (signing that device out) or sign out everywhere.
  • Approve only friends you know. Connect and invite links can be passed on, so each one only asks: nothing is shared until you approve, and you can check the site address first.
  • Photo locations are removed from photos you post, and left out of a Facebook import unless you choose to include them: they can show where you live.
  • Keep it up to date from the Update page (marked when there's a new version), so security fixes reach your site.

The installer downloads afterwrd __VERSION__ from its published release and checks every file against the fingerprint __MANIFEST_SHORT__… before installing anything.
